Haircare Tips

Discover the best practices for keeping your hair healthy and vibrant.

Understanding whether your hair is straight, wavy, curly, or coily can help you choose the right products and routine.

Know Your Hair Type

Washing your hair too often can strip it of natural oils. Aim for 2-3 times a week unless you have very oily hair.

Avoid Over-Washing

Frequent use of heat tools like blow dryers, straighteners, and curling irons can damage your hair. Use them sparingly and always apply a heat protectant

Limit Heat Styling

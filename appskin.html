skincare routine based on your skin type

Normal Skin

  • Cleanse with a gentle cleanser twice a day.
  • Use a hydrating toner to balance the skin.
  • Apply a lightweight moisturizer.
  • Use sunscreen daily to protect against UV damage.
  • Exfoliate once a week to remove dead skin cells.

Oily Skin

  • Cleanse with a foaming or gel cleanser twice a day.
  • Use a toner with salicylic acid to control oil.
  • Apply a lightweight, oil-free moisturizer.
  • Use a mattifying sunscreen during the day.
  • Exfoliate 2-3 times a week to prevent clogged pores.

Dry Skin

  • Cleanse with a creamy or hydrating cleanser.
  • Use a hydrating toner to lock in moisture.
  • Apply a rich, hydrating moisturizer.
  • Use a hydrating sunscreen during the day.
  • Apply a hydrating mask once a week.

Combination Skin

  • Cleanse with a gentle foaming cleanser.
  • Use a balancing toner for both oily and dry areas.
  • Apply a lightweight moisturizer on oily areas and a richer one on dry areas.
  • Use sunscreen suitable for combination skin.
  • Exfoliate 1-2 times a week, focusing on oily areas.

Sensitive Skin

  • Cleanse with a gentle, fragrance-free cleanser.
  • Use a soothing toner with calming ingredients.
  • Apply a gentle, hypoallergenic moisturizer.
  • Use a mineral sunscreen for sensitive skin.
  • Avoid exfoliating too often; once every two weeks is sufficient.

Here are some general skincare tips to help maintain healthy skin

By incorporating these tips into your daily routine, you can help ensure your skin stays healthy, vibrant, and protected.

Identify whether your skin is normal, oily, dry, combination, or sensitive. This will help you choose the right products and routine.

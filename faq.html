It depends on your hair type and lifestyle. Generally, washing 2-3 times a week is sufficient for most hair types. If your hair is oily, you may need to wash more frequently.

Avoid excessive heat styling, use a heat protectant, limit chemical treatments, and keep your hair moisturized with conditioners or masks.
Use a moisturizing shampoo and conditioner, apply anti-frizz serum or oil, and avoid towel-drying your hair. Instead, use a microfiber towel or let it air dry.
Look for natural oils (like argan or coconut oil), proteins (like keratin), and vitamins (like biotin and vitamin E) that promote healthy hair.
Maintain a healthy diet, avoid tight hairstyles, massage your scalp regularly, and use products with ingredients like castor oil or rosemary oil that are known to promote growth.

Start by maintaining a balanced diet, staying hydrated, getting regular exercise, and ensuring adequate sleep. It's also important to manage stress and avoid harmful habits like smoking and excessive alcohol consumption.

The general recommendation is to drink about 8 glasses (64 ounces) of water a day. However, this can vary based on factors like age, gender, activity level, and climate.
A balanced diet includes a variety of foods: fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats. It limits processed foods, sugars, and unhealthy fats. Portion control is also key.
Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity or 75 minutes of vigorous activity per week, along with muscle-strengthening activities on two or more days a week.
